# Pond fish credits

Current fish: **Koi Fish** by **7PLUS**, licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
Source: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/koi-fish-236859b809984f52b70c94fd040b9c59

Adaptation in Blender: removed baked animation, welded split vertices, subdivided the connected silhouette, narrowed the body and retained the original colour and scale/fin normal maps. Runtime body and tail motion, pectoral motion, local mouth movement, coat variations and feeding behaviour were added. Normal strength was reduced to preserve small scale relief.
