# 大观园人物志 · 标准像提示词

以后画同一人物，沿用下面的「人物外貌/服饰」段落，只换动作和场景。

## 通用风格前缀（每张都加）

```
Chinese gongbi fine-line painting with light color wash (工笔淡彩) on aged xuan rice paper texture, delicate firm ink outlines, elegant muted pastel palette, Ming-style aoqun clothing (cross-collar or stand-collar jacket with narrow sleeves fitted to the wrist + floor-length pleated mamian skirt); no Tang high-waist skirt, no wide sleeves, no shawl/披帛, no ribbons, no kimono, no Manchu headdress. Background fully painted edge to edge, no blank paper, no glow or backlight. Horizontal 16:9 character portrait, three-quarter length, [facing front | three-quarter view], face clearly and finely drawn.
```

## 通用结尾（每张都加）

```
Natural anatomy, correct hands. No text, no calligraphy, no seals, no signature, no watermark.
```

## 动作规矩

- 一次只做一件事，手和道具的拿法要合理，不要一边扶着东西一边干别的。
- 画完先自检：动作别扭、手指画坏、服饰不对的，重画后再交。
- 想要满幅画面时加 `full-bleed edge-to-edge composition with no border or frame`，避免两侧出现纸边（元春那张就是这样修好的）。

## 贾宝玉 baoyu.webp

```
Jia Baoyu from Dream of the Red Chamber, a handsome youth of about 15: face round and bright like the mid-autumn full moon, complexion fresh like spring-dawn flowers, crisp temples, ink-black brows, eyes like autumn ripples, a gentle smile. Hair gathered in a topknot bound by a small purple-gold crown inlaid with gems, a gold forehead band with two dragons playing with a pearl. He wears a bright red Qing-style archery robe (箭袖袍): LONG sleeves reaching all the way to the wrists, sleeves narrow and close-fitting along the whole arm, ending in curved horse-hoof cuffs that cover the backs of his hands; robe embroidered with gold butterflies among flowers; a five-colored silk belt with long tassels. Around his neck a gold necklace and a five-colored cord holding an egg-sized lustrous oval white jade. Pose: standing relaxed, both arms hanging naturally, hands loosely clasped in front. Setting: Yihong Courtyard — broad banana plants on one side, a crabapple tree with pink-red blossoms on the other, carved lattice windows and veranda behind. No short sleeves, no wide sleeves.
```
三分之一侧面。注意：一定要写 `LONG sleeves… narrow… horse-hoof cuffs` 和 `No short sleeves, no wide sleeves`，否则容易画成大袖或短袖。

## 全局规矩（2026-10-08 起，以后每张都适用）

- 风格：工笔淡彩、宣纸质感、16:9、无文字无水印；只用淡彩，不要发光、逆光、夕阳。
- 服饰：全部明制袄裙（交领或立领短袄/长袄 + 马面裙，窄袖或中袖）。禁止唐制高腰裙、大袖披帛、和服、满族大拉翅。
- 脸：每个人的脸型、眉眼、神态都要不同，不要一张古典美人通用脸。
- 手：一只手只拿一件东西，提示词里要写清楚怎么握。动作自然，一次只做一件事。
- 背景：画满，不留空白纸。
- 尺寸：1600×900 或更大（生成原图约 1280×720，交付前用 Lanczos 放大到 1920×1080）。
- 画完放大自查手和脸，动作怪、手指崩、服饰穿越就重画再交。
- 防跑偏负面词（必加）：narrow sleeves fitted to the wrist, floor-length mamian skirt to the ankles, no wide sleeves, no shawl/披帛, no floating ribbons, no blank paper.
